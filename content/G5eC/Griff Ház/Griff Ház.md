A Griff Ház kétségtelenül Hűvöskő legpatinásabb kalandozócéhe, a legendák szerint még akkor alapították amikor a Szigettenger ezen része a Nagy Császárság uralma alatt állt. Bár ez a hozzáértők számára túlzásnak tűnhet (hisz közel 3 évezreddel ezelőtti időszakról van szó), de a céh kétségtelenül emberemlékezet óta jelen van a szigeten.

A Griff-ház létezése három pilléren áll:
1. összetartás, 
2. lokálpatriótizmus (politikától függetlenül)
3. együttműködés a hatalommal, törvények betartása (még ha nem is értünk vele egyet)
A beavatási rituálénál el kell mondani az esküt, miszerint hűségesek leszünk a Griff-házhoz és a bajtársainkhoz benne. És megőrizzük a ház titkait.

#### **The ranking:**

***Acel ~~Iron~~ Tier.*** The greenhorns of adventuring, tasked with duties of a menial nature, from mundane errands to minor guard duties.
These individuals form the first stepping stone to a greater calling. 
Typical Character level: 1 to 2 (CR ⅛ to . NPCs)

***Copper Tier.*** These are the bulwarks against the common threats against the simple people. Copper-ranked heroes are seen as proficient
in dealing with lesser monsters, such as harpies and specters, or organized threats like goblin and gnoll raiders.
Typical Character level: 3 to 4 (CR . to 1 NPCs)

Orion

***Bronze Tier.*** The professional face of adventuring, these individuals bear the weight of combating significant monstrous threats,
such as the petrifying basilisks and the dangerous manticores. Their reputation is one of reliability and strength.
Typical Character level: 5 to 6 (CR 2 to 4 NPCs)

***Silver Tier.*** Local champions, those of the Silver rank, are already considered true heroes. They are the ones who battle against
foes likes trolls, extraplanar elementals, and even wyverns. Their names are echoed in the towns and cities they protect.
Typical Character level: 7 to 10 (CR 5 to 7 NPCs)

***Gold Tier.*** The epitome of mortal prowess, these individuals are tasked to face the worst terrors. Capable of challenging fearsome demons, powerful emoguns, ancient vampires, and even capable of dealing with the occasional maddened adult dragon. They serve as an inspiration to the populace and their names are known
*Typical Character level: 11 to 16 (CR 8 to 13 NPCs)*

***Diamond Tier.*** The paragons of heroism and living legends, their deeds have carved their names into the annals of Tanares.
The Diamond rank is more than a measure of ability; it is the embodiment of legendary heroism that transcends conventional
scales. **Avelum, Sedura, Garion, and Sir Erick** are some of the individuals that embody the diamond rank, their power considered
immeasurable. These individuals are called only to deal with situations of world shattering consequences.
*Typical Character level: 17+ (CR 14+ NPCs)*
