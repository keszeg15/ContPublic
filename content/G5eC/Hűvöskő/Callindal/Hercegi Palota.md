The _Palazzo del Principe_ rises from the terraced cliffs above Calindal’s opalescent bay, its domed towers and rose-marble arches gleaming in the sun. Once a fortress, now a symbol of dynastic power, the palace blends opulence with strategic foresight. Lush terraced gardens descend to the city below, while within, the air is perfumed with incense and old secrets.

# **Notable Features and Rooms:**

- **Napterem:** Grand audience chamber with a gilded dome, mosaics of ancient battles, and a throne carved from driftwood and gold.   
- **Vigilarium:** A central observation tower, formerly a lighthouse, now a watch post manned by elite guards. Offers a full view of Calindal and the harbor.
- **Arnyektorony:** A restricted wing housing secret records, arcane artifacts, and occasionally prisoners of state.
- **Fuggokertek:** Alchemical gardens with medicinal and magical plants. Protected by enchanted fauna.
- **Karmazsin Galeria:** Long hall filled with ancestral portraits and trapped artworks. Whispered to be haunted.
- **Virellus Katakombai:** Subterranean passages named after a mad prince who vanished within them. Now used by spies, smugglers, and the prince's hidden agents.
