#### **The Bronze Witch**
###### **Description**
A clay building with a slate roof, the Bronze Witch stands four-stories tall. This place is usually only busy during the evening hours. Inside, the tavern's central lounge contains a blazing hearth.
As you approach, you notice the tavern has an angry atmosphere. A group of goblins has stolen the last shipment of alcohol.
###### **Notable Patrons**
**Marcyn Spicer**: An elegant male human is sitting at the bar, making notes on something in the tavern.
**Forge** : An old male genasi is sitting near the fireplace, talking quietly to someone else.
**Gamath Whitethistle**: A powerful male elf is sitting near the fireplace, reading a stack of documents.
**Vhoadan Nharimlur**: A beautiful male elf is sitting at a table, telling an exciting story to a few other patrons.

 ###### **Rumors, Gossip and Hearsay**
- Several disappearances were noticed after a strange ship left port the other night. Just talk, no one important is missing.
- Someone wrote a derogatory phrase on the royal palace door in red paint and the king is personally seeking revenge for this act.
- A family friend was seen fraternising with the prince and is now pregnant. The king wants her executed, but the queen wants the prince to marry her before she has the child.
######  **Meals**
**Breakfast:** Thin porridge and chewy bacon, with a side of cherries.
**Lunch:** Grilled halibut and coconut, with crocodile sausage on the side.
**Dinner:** Sauteed marlin and red olives, with garlic bread on the side.
