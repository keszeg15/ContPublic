TACTICS

**Before Combat** Lieutenant Kujara uses his hexes to protect

allies (and himself ) with *ward*.

**During Combat** A talented tactician, Kujara targets

spellcasters first, targeting the squares they are in with area

spells. If a warrior interrupts him the witch uses *blindness* or

*confusion* to get them out of the way, and if a spellcaster is

proving too difficult to down, he uses *feeblemind*. Otherwise

Kujara delights in unleashing elemental forces on his

enemies, and has been known to use *transformation* simply to

rush and eliminate rogue opponents (typically the ones that

survive his magical onslaught).

**Morale** Kujara uses *teleport* to escape when reduced to 15

hp or less.
