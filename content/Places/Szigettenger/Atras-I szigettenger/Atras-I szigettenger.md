# Appendix

https://www.enworld.org/threads/rpg-evolution-gilded-veils-and-high-seas-sails.719433/

Anyway I did a series of adventures set in Melaka and the Majapahit Empire and a strong Mercantile campaign inspired by the Ayyavole and other South Indian trade guilds that operated between the 9th and 14th centuries.  
  
In particular my "500 Lords of the Vaniyaga" Campaign (unfinished) comprised a network of interlocked trade networks spread across the coastal ports of the Mahasegara from Outremer to the Straits of Melaka to the Old Continent, all bound by the Vira Balanju Dharma (the Law of Heroic Traders) and the Dan Nakya (Council of Auditors).The climate of the region is largely tropical and the peoples have been blessed with rich foods and spices, perfumes, gemstones, textiles and all manner of beasts.  
  
The two emblems of the Vaniyaga are the White Umbrella, carried by the Heroic Traders when conducting business with the rulers of the nations in which they trade or public temple processions; and the 7-headed Naga Raja used on official seals of the Vaniyaga and as an entranceway to many Vaniyaga built Temples.  
  
Anyway the PCs started in the Port of Tanjuvar in Pandya Nadu where they are invested into the Vira Balanja and commission by the Patanaswami to travel to the Port of Takola in Shangri La (home port of the Gokhong Apemen) to collect a new Jong (ship) which they will then deliver to the Port of Melaka and establish a new Trading House in order to build further trade opportunities with markets across the Old Continent.  
  
  
PC Options:  
The Vira Balanja (Heroic Traders) includes  

- Exporers & Diplomats who actively seek new opportunities, exploring by land and sea and developing relationships in each new port they visit.
- Builders & Merchants who establish a Trade center for participation in local markets under the Vira Balanja Dharma (Law of fair trade, cooperation and generous giving)
- Priests & Warriors as the Vira Balanja provides mutual support and protection against piracy and barbarians. They also assist with building temples, canals and irrigation networks.
- The Dan Nakya (Council of Auditors) charged with enforcing the laws of the Vira Banaju Dharma, viewing the records of business, identifying cheaters, and protecting both buyers and sellers.

  
Anyway after dealing with some stolen guild seals and securing a crew in Tanjuvar, the PCs sailed to Takola and discovered in under attack by pirate raiders. The PCs assist the local Gokhong, venture in to the jungle and rescue prisoners before paying tribute the temple of Hanuman Vayuputra.  
  
Defeating the raiders and securing the Jong, the PCs traveled south, did some trade and minor exploration, gained a stowaway princess, explored a haunted island and then encountered a Sea Dragon who demanded tribute before letting them pass into the Straits of Melaka.  
  
We got to the Port of Melaka and negotiated with the Raja to build a Trading House but had to deal with some spirits first. Unfortunately the adventure then went dormant...
