# AdelBatran
## Overview
**Political Region**: Territory
#Political-Region/Territory

[[Adelfia]] szomszédai, a környék államai, városai:

- Atras Császárság és Protektorátus: az Atras [[Szigettenger]] urai, Adelfiától nyugatra-északnyugatra
- [[Újvilág]]: Új Provincia és a Független Államok Közössége - északra a Kristály Sivatagon túl. 
- Vishnara (Hét Nemzet): az Atras [[Szigettenger]] legészakkeletibb szigetcsoportjai, amelyek emberemlékezet óta hét különböző nemzet uralma alatt állnak, bár ezek összetétele gyakran változik. A misztikus hetes szám oka hét darab mágikusan igen aktív gyümölcsfa, amely meghatározza a környék politikáját. Jelenlegi befolyásos államok: Ajatar, Karmazsin Emírátusok, Kereskedő Hercegségek, Alton Oir Magiokrácia, Tíz Sárkány Királysága, Hsigo, Hegyi Királyság.
- Tirenmer: mágus-diktatúra városállam, északra a tengerparton
- Beyoman: Hegyoldalakból kifaragott város az Efiras folyó két partján, amelynek egykori dicsősége mára már jócskán megkopott
- Batigor: szigorú kasztrendszerrel biró rabszolgatartó városállam, jelentős tea-ültetvényekkel
- Magahon: a környék első meghatározó ember-birodalmának fővárosa, ma már csak egy városállam. Híres csonkagúla alakú épületeiről, az itt lakók szakáll-kompozícióiról, furcsa süvegeiről, és khoepsh-es paladinjairól.
- Mersani Birodalom: dekadens  Markon ([[Marcho]]) államvallasú ország, amely a történelem során egykor uralta az egész vidéket. Érdekessége, hogy a szabad emberek nem mutatják meg az arcukat, csak a rabszolgákét látni. Az alsóbb kasztbeliek kendővel, a felsőbbiek maszkkal fedik el az arcukat, amelyekről leolvasható a rang/élettörténet/legyőzött ellenfelek. A Titkos Városokban még ma is dekadens pompa uralkodik, de az ország nagy részére már pusztai nomád/rabló életforma a jellemző.
- Pikkely: a pikkelyes fajták (yuan-ti, gyíkember, naga, tetrani, keth, bullywug, grung, grippli) városa. Az utóbbi időben újra jelentős erővé kezd válni. Adelfiától délre a parton, az Efiras deltájánál elterülő mocsár szélén.
- Goblin Emírségek
- Tűzóriás város az Irha Hegységben.
- Az Úszó Város: az Efiras folyón utazik föl-le

Name: Aidalis Valentine

Species: Human

Class: Commoner/Experienced Alchemist

Occupation: Alchemist; offers services to anyone who needs him and owns

a small shop in his house where he sells materials for alchemy and

magic.

Gender: Male

Age: 26

Height: 6'0"

Weight: 160 pounds

Hair: Grown out to about chin-length, bangs combed over the right eye at

all times, because of burns caused by a fire when he was younger. Color

is scarlet red.

Eyes: Light green. 

Attire: Mostly dresses in shades of grey, white, black, and pale blue.

Almost always wears his high-collared grey trenchcoat.

Alignment: Neutral Good 

Demeanour: Normally friendly because of his business habits, and also

very honest and straightforward. Doesn't bother with preamble, but does

like to show off his skills every now and then. But then, who doesn't?

His honesty can be very brutal. And like all dedicated smokers, he's

miserable if he doesn't get his cigarette when he wants it.

If there's a task to do or goal to reach, he'll do it no matter what.

Unless it involves someone's life. He's a very reliable person.

Skills & Abilities: Well...He's a skilled alchemist and uses every bit

of it to his advantage. He also has partial control of the elements,

(fire, wind, water, earth..)and can be considered sort of a mage. He's

great with using the earth and trees to his advantage, and can turn them

either into shields or weapons. In a sense, he's a bit like a

nature-speaker.

Weaknesses: Well...He's human, and very mortal, and prone to mistakes.

Isn't that weakness enough? 

Weapons: When he can't use the elements or alchemy or they're just not

very conveniant, he relies on his steel crossbow and poison-tipped

arrows. The poison's lethal enough to infect even a lycanthrope for a

period of time, depending on its rate of breakdown of toxins; it won't

kill anyone, but it will induce flu-like effects for a few days on

anyone without immunity.

Magical Items: A smaller, secondary silver crossbow especially for

lycanthropes and vampires. It's made from alchemy to cause the most

damage possible; can induce extreme flu effects in lycanthropes,

regardless of immunity to poisons. To vampires, it reduces mental powers

for a period of time, depending on the vamp's resistance.

Family: His younger brother Ashilia, parents are dead, rest of family is

scattered throughout the world. 

History: His parents were the victims of a fire when he was younger, and

he has personally raised and cared for his younger brother Ashilia.

He hates vampires with a passion, because they're the ones that caused

the fire in the first place.

            Where the deposit of lapis lazuli occurs, the valley of the Kokcha is about 200 yards wide. On both sides the mountains are high and naked. The entrance to the mines is in the face of the mountain, on the right bank of the stream, and about 1,500 feet above its level. The formation is of a black and white limestone, unstratified, though plentifully veined with lines. The summit off the mountains is rugged, and their sides destitute of soil or vegetation. The path by which the mines are approached is steep and dangerous, the effect of neglect rather than natural difficulties. The mountains have been tried for the lapis lazuli at various places; but the following is a section of the principal and latest worked mine:

            "The shaft by which you descend to the gallery is about ten feet square, and is not so perpendicular as to prevent your walking down. The gallery is eighty paces long, with a gentle descent, but it terminates in a hole twenty feet in diameter and as many deep. The width and height of the gallery, though irregular, may be estimated at about twelve feet; but at some places where the roof has fallen in its section is so contracted that the visitor is forced to advance on his hands and knees. Accidents would appear to have been frequent, and one place in the mine is named after some unhappy sufferers who were crushed by the falling roof. No precaution has been taken to support by means of pillars the top of the mine, which, formed of detached rocks wedged together, requires only a little more lateral expansion to drop into the cavity. Any further operations can only be carried on at the most imminent risk to the miners. The temperature at the further end of the mine was 36ş of Fahrenheit, while in the open air at its entrance it was 29ş.

            "The method of extracting the lapis is sufficiently simple. Under the spot to be quarried a fire is kindled, and its flame, fed by dry furze(1), is made to flicker over the surface. When the rock has become sufficiently soft, or to use the workmen’s expression, nurim, it is beaten with hammers, and flake after flake knocked off until the stone of which they are in search is discovered. Deep grooves are then picked out round the lapis-lazuli, into which crow-bars are inserted, and the stone and part of its matrix are detached.

            The workmen enumerate three descriptions of ladjword. These are the Neeli, or indigo color; the Asmani, or light blue; and the Suvsi, or green. There relative value is in the order in which I have mentioned them. The richest colours are found in the darkest rock, and the nearer the river the greater is said to be the purity of the stone. The search for ladjword is only prosecuted during winter, probably because, labour in the mine being compulsory, the inhabitants are less injured by giving it in a season of comparative idleness than when the fields require their attention. Perhaps, also, during the cold of winter the rock may be more susceptible to the action of heat, and thus more easily reduced, than when its temperature is higher. Within the last four years Murad Beg(2) has ceased to work both the lapis-lazuli and ruby mines; and the reason assigned for his altered policy is the small success which has hitherto attended the operations. \[Wood (1872)]
