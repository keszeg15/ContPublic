[Members of House Uskevren](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Members_of_House_Uskevren) 

The **Uskevren** (meaning "too bold to hide" in some forgotten tongue) were a noble family of [Selgaunt](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Selgaunt) who lived primarily in [Stormweather Towers](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Uskevren). Their house badge was a gold horse-head-and-fouled-anchor. Their money originally stockpiled through piracy, the family was nearly destroyed in the 1330s [DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/DR) by rival families who discovered their illicit means of money-making.[<sup>\[1]</sup>](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Uskevren#cite_note-THoS-0) 

The only surviving family member, [Thamalon Uskevren](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Uskevren), rebuilt the family fortune by buying and selling land, and then began sponsoring common folk. After this, the people of Selgaunt began to trust the Uskevren name again[<sup>\[1]</sup>](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Uskevren#cite_note-THoS-0)

## **Properties[ Edit](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Uskevren?action=edit&section=3)**
In addition to Stormweather Towers, the Uskevrens had a tallhouse near the [Way of the Manticore](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Way_of_the_Manticore) and another on [Lurvin Street](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Uskevren). ## **Magical Items [Edit](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Uskevren?action=edit&section=4)**
Throughout the years, when there was doubt if someone was truly an Uskevren, the family used a magical cup called the *[Quaff of the Uskevren](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Uskevren)*. Enchanted long ago by [Helemgaularn of the Seven Lightnings](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Uskevren) to keep revelers from stealing his mead, its enchantments were later altered so that only one of the blood of the Uskevren could touch it bare-handed and not be instantly burned.[<sup>\[1]</sup>](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Uskevren#cite_note-THoS-0)
