## 🕱 **Pestilence Domain**

_“All life rots. I am merely the hand that hastens its return to the soil.”_

Clerics of Pestilence serve deities of decay, corruption, disease, and poisonous transformation. They see sickness and rot not as evil, but as the world’s method of balance — tearing down the strong to feed the weak. Their prayers are whispered in swamps, plague pits, and fungal groves where life and death intermingle.

### 🩸 **Bonus Proficiencies**

When you choose this domain at 1st level, you gain proficiency with **poisoner’s kits** and **heavy armor**.  
You also learn the **Poison Spray** cantrip if you don’t already know it.

---

### ☠️ **Rotbringer (1st Level)**

When you hit a creature with a weapon attack or damaging cantrip, you can infuse it with divine toxins.  
The target takes extra poison damage equal to your **Wisdom modifier (minimum 1)**.
You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **proficiency bonus**, regaining all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
> _Your touch spreads unseen corruption._

---
### 🌫️ **Aura of Miasma (2nd Level)**

Starting at 2nd level, you can use your Channel Divinity to exude an aura of disease and decay:
- As an action, you emit a **10-foot-radius cloud** of pestilence for 1 minute.
- Each creature of your choice that starts its turn in the aura must make a **Constitution saving throw** or take **poison damage equal to 2d8 + your Wisdom modifier** and be **poisoned until the start of your next turn**.
- While active, plants and food within the aura wither instantly.
> _Your divine presence makes the air itself infectious._

---
### 🕯️ **Rotten Resilience (6th Level)**

You have adapted to your god’s corruption.  
You gain **resistance to poison and necrotic damage**, and you are **immune to the poisoned condition**.
Additionally, when you cast a spell that deals poison or necrotic damage, you can add your **Wisdom modifier** to one damage roll of that spell.

---
### 🪱 **Divine Strike (8th Level)**

Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can deal an extra **1d8 poison damage**.  
This increases to **2d8** at 14th level.
> _Even a scratch from your weapon festers with divine contagion._

---
### 🌋 **Avatar of Blight (17th Level)**

You become a walking epidemic. As an action, you can activate your **Blighted Form** for 1 minute:
- You gain **immunity** to poison and necrotic damage.
- Any creature that hits you with a melee attack takes **1d8 poison damage**.
- At the start of each of your turns, choose up to **three creatures** within 30 feet; they must succeed on a **Constitution saving throw** or take **4d8 poison damage** and be **poisoned** for 1 minute.  
    They can repeat the save at the end of each of their turns.
Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest.
> _You are the walking plague — a living blight upon the world._
